    Big balcony. Covers the whole living-room roof plus the extension roof, about 25 m², opening off all three bedrooms. The roof needs strengthening and waterproofing, and a street-facing balcony may raise privacy questions with council.

    Juliet balconies. Each bedroom window becomes full-height doors with a metal rail just outside, so the doors open wide for air and the view but nobody steps out. The roof below stays a plain roof. The cost per balcony is my own estimate (doors $3.5k–7k, rail $1.5k–3k, flashing $0.5k–1k).
